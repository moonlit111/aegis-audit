Tool capabilities:
- upx: UPX only; produces a separate unpacked image.
- floss: FLOSS 3.1.1 recovers decoded/stack/tight strings from PE by static analysis and emulation; it does not undo arbitrary control-flow flattening, MBA or virtualization.

Inspect changed-function counts and matched rules; completion with no changes is not successful deobfuscation.
Never count renaming, readable noise, a planned step or mere tool completion as successful recovery. Keep unsupported protections and uncertainty explicit.
